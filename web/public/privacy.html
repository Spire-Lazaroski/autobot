Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what the Spire Analytics app at spire.ristesky.com (the "app") stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. The app is a small, invite-only paper-trading test. It keeps as little as it needs.

1. Who is responsible

Spire Analytics, operated by Spire Lazaroski · spire.lazaroski@gmail.com

2. What the app stores

WhatWhy
Your user name, when you joined, the date of your last sign-in, and the note the operator wrote on your inviteTo run your account and the invite list
Your password, only as a one-way hash (PBKDF2); the password itself is never storedTo check your sign-in
Your permission for one Alpaca paper account (an Alpaca access token, or your paper API keys), stored encrypted, and the last four digits of that account's numberSo your bot can trade your paper account
Your paper account's balance, positions, orders and the bot's decisionsTo run your bot and show you your pages and reports
A sign-in cookie (and, with "keep me signed in", for up to 30 days)To keep you signed in; no tracking or advertising cookies are used
Your network address, briefly in memory only, after wrong passwordsTo block repeated guessing (cleared after 15 minutes)

The app never receives your Alpaca password: with "Connect with Alpaca" you sign in on Alpaca's own page. The app does not ask for, and does not store, your real name, address, bank details or identity documents.

3. Who else sees it

  • Alpaca (Alpaca Securities / Alpaca Markets): your bot's orders go to your Alpaca paper account. Alpaca's own privacy policy covers what Alpaca does with your account.
  • The hosting provider that runs the server for the operator, only as needed to run it.
  • The operator can see the user list (names, dates) and, to fix problems, your bot's logs and paper results.

Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

4. How long it is kept

As long as your account exists. When you disconnect, the app deletes its Alpaca permission at once. When you ask the operator to delete your account, your user record, permission and bot data are deleted within 30 days (backups included as they roll over).

5. Your rights

You can ask to see the data the app holds about you, to correct it, to receive a copy, or to delete it, and you can object to its use. Write to the address above. If you live in the EU or a country with similar rules you may also complain to your data-protection authority.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS; passwords are hashed; Alpaca permissions are encrypted; repeated wrong passwords are blocked; users can see only their own account. No system is perfectly secure: please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
